### Undergraduates

There are several routes into the group for undergraduates at {{ site.university }}: part-time or summer employment, enrolling in a directed-research course (PHYS 3993, 3994, 5993, or 5994), completing an honors thesis through the [University Honors Program](https://honors.umn.edu/prospective-students/why-uhp), joining the university-wide [Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP)](https://ugresearch.umn.edu/opportunities/urop) or [Researcher-Initiated Student Employment (RISE)](https://ugresearch.umn.edu/research-opportunities/researcher-initiated-student-employment-rise) program. If any of these sound like a good fit, please reach out directly — no prior research experience is required.


### PhD Students

Prospective PhD students should apply through the [Astrophysics Graduate Program admissions process](https://cse.umn.edu/mifa/grad/apply) at the {{ site.university }}, and are welcome to reach out in advance to learn about our research and potential opportunities to join the group.


### Postdocs

We're glad to help support a candidate bringing their own fellowship, including the [51 Pegasi b Fellowship](https://www.hsfoundation.org/programs/science/51-pegasi-b-fellowship/), [NASA Hubble Fellowship Program (NHFP)](https://www.stsci.edu/stsci-research/fellowships/nasa-hubble-fellowship-program), or [NSF Astronomy and Astrophysics Postdoctoral Fellowship (AAPF)](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/aapf-nsf-astronomy-astrophysics-postdoctoral-fellowships). Open postdoc positions (when available) are posted on the [AAS Job Register](https://jobregister.aas.org/).
